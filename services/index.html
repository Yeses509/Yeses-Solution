Frequently asked questions

How long does a website take?

One-page websites take 3–5 days. Business websites usually take about 14 days, and full online stores up to 30 days.

How do payments work?

50% to start and 50% when your website is delivered — written into a clear contract before we begin.

Do I own the website?

Yes. After final payment, the design and code are yours.

Which languages can my website be in?

English, Arabic and Kurdish with full right-to-left support — more languages can be added.

Is support included after launch?

Yes — 30 days of free support after delivery, then the optional Care Plan ($10/month, cancel anytime) covering updates, daily backups, security monitoring and small content edits.

Will my website work on phones?

Yes. Every site is designed mobile-first and tested on real phones before delivery.
