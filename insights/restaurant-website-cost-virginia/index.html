What a restaurant website must include

Whichever level you choose, a restaurant website only earns its keep if it does four jobs:

  • A menu people can read — fast-loading pages with prices and photos. Not a PDF download guests have to pinch and zoom.
  • Reservations without friction — one tap starts a WhatsApp booking with date, time and party size already filled in; your team confirms it.
  • Hours and directions where customers search — connected properly to Google and Maps.
  • Your languages — Virginia's restaurant scene is multilingual. Our sites are built in English, Arabic and Kurdish with proper right-to-left support, and more languages can be added for $100 each.
