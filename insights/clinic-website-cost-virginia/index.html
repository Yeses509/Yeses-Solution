What a clinic website must include

Whichever level you choose, a clinic website only earns its keep if it does four jobs:

  • Service pages patients can scan — what each treatment is, who it suits, and what to expect, written in plain language.
  • Booking without friction — a guided request flow: the patient picks a service and a time, sends it, and your team confirms. No phone tag.
  • Trust on the first screen — your credentials, your real reviews, your address and hours where patients look for them.
  • Your languages — Virginia's patient base is multilingual. Our sites are built in English, Arabic and Kurdish with proper right-to-left support, and more languages can be added for $100 each.

Frequently asked questions

How much should a clinic or med spa budget for a website?

Most clinics and med spas in Virginia are well served by a $499–$999 website: a one-page site with services, hours and a booking button at $499, or a full multi-page site at $999. Budget $2,999 only if you also want to sell products or gift cards online.

Can patients book appointments from the website?

Yes. We build a booking-request flow — like the one on The MedSpa by RayRay's website — where the patient picks a service and a time and sends a pre-filled request; your team confirms it. Nothing is presented as a confirmed booking until you approve it.

How long does a clinic website take to build?

A one-page clinic website takes 3–5 days. A full multi-page website usually takes about 14 days, and a site with a full online store can take up to 30 days.

Do I own my clinic's website?

Yes. At YESES SOLUTION, after final payment the design and code are yours — written into the contract before work begins. Payments are 50% to start and 50% on delivery.

What does a clinic website need besides the design?

A domain name ($40 for 1 year, $75 for 3 years or $100 for 5 years through us) and hosting, which we set up and connect for you. After launch, the optional Care Plan is $10/month (cancel anytime) or $7/month billed $84/year, covering updates, daily backups, security monitoring and small content edits.
